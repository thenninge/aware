# 🎨 AI Prompts for Cold Bore Aware App Ikon

## 📱 Hovedprompt (Detaljert)

```
Create a modern, professional iOS app icon for "Cold Bore Aware" - a hunting planning app focused on line-of-sight analysis and safe shooting placement.

Visual concept:
- Central element: A stylized rifle scope crosshair or reticle symbol
- Background: Subtle topographic contour lines or mountain silhouette
- Accent: Small awareness indicator (eye symbol or warning triangle)
- Color palette: Deep blue (#1e40af) as primary, with forest green (#16a34a) and slate gray accents
- Style: Flat design with subtle gradient, minimalist, professional
- Mood: Precision, safety, outdoor adventure, tactical awareness

Technical requirements:
- 1024x1024 pixels
- No text or small details (must be readable at 60x60px)
- Rounded corners will be applied by iOS
- Clean, bold shapes that work at small sizes
- No transparency (solid background)

Design must convey:
1. Precision shooting/hunting
2. Safety and awareness (avoid buildings/populated areas)
3. Outdoor/mountain terrain
4. Professional tool for serious hunters

Inspiration: Combine elements of outdoor navigation apps with tactical/precision shooting aesthetics.
```

## 📐 Design Prinsipper for App Ikoner

### ✅ DO:
- Bruk bold, enkle former
- Maksimalt 2-3 farger
- Sentraliser hovedelement
- Test ved 60x60px størrelse
- Fokus på gjenkjennelighet

### ❌ DON'T:
- Inkluder tekst eller tall
- Bruk komplekse detaljer
- Ha for mange elementer
- Bruk gradient som primær feature (kan være subtil)
- Kopier eksisterende ikoner direkte

---

## 🔄 Iterasjonsprosess

1. **Generer 4-6 varianter** med ulike konsepter
2. **Test i ulike størrelser**:
   ```
   - 1024x1024 (original)
   - 180x180 (iPhone)
   - 120x120 (iPad)
   - 60x60 (Settings)
   ```
3. **Få feedback** fra potensielle brukere
4. **Refiner** basert på feedback
5. **Eksporter** i alle nødvendige størrelser
